Bran Castle

Bran Castle, also known as Dracula's Castle, is a national monument and landmark in Romania. Surrounded by an aura of mystery and legend, the castle is a must-visit for anyone exploring the history and culture of this beautiful country. Built in the 14th century, it stands at the border between Transylvania and Wallachia, and offers a breathtaking view of the Carpathian Mountains.
The castle has a rich history, having been used by royals, soldiers, and as a strategic fortress. Today, it functions as a museum open to tourists, displaying art and furniture collected by Queen Marie of Romania. As you wander through its narrow passages and hidden rooms, you'll get a sense of the castle's storied past and its place in both historical and modern culture.

Peleș Castle is a Neo-Renaissance castle in the Carpathian Mountains, near Sinaia, in Prahova County, Romania. Built between 1873 and 1914, the castle was commissioned by King Carol I and serves as one of the most beautiful castles in Europe. It features an architectural blend of Gothic Revival, Renaissance, and Baroque styles.
The castle's interior is stunning, with numerous rooms adorned with intricate woodwork, exquisite tapestries, and an impressive collection of arms and armor. The stunning frescoes and stained glass windows add to the castle's opulence. Visitors can explore the grand halls, private chambers, and picturesque courtyards while learning about the rich history and royal heritage of Romania.
